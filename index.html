About Daniel

I'm Daniel Wong, founder of Daniel Workshop, where I craft iOS applications guided by a simple philosophy: technology should feel human.

In a world of feature-bloated apps and dark patterns, I choose a different path. Every line of code I write serves a purpose, every interface element earns its place, and every user interaction is designed to feel natural and delightful.

My journey began many years ago with a fascination for the intersection of beautiful design and powerful functionality. What started as curiosity has evolved into a deep commitment to creating apps that respect your time, understand your needs, and enhance your daily life without getting in the way.

I believe the best technology is invisible - it works so well you forget it's there. That's the standard I hold myself to with every project at Daniel Workshop.
